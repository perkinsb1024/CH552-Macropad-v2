Connecting reads one sample. Monitoring is optional and off by default. For untouched idle testing, disconnect the reader, leave the device powered, then reconnect and read the retained watermark. Record any fault before reconnecting: USB reconfiguration can change runtime state.

The watermark includes startup after C initialization, normal operation, USB interrupts and diagnostic request handling. Readout adds no call frame and briefly masks interrupts. The peak is the largest sample observed during this connection; downloaded results retain earlier connections too. Power cycling or restarting firmware resets the device watermark.

Pattern collisions can undercount usage. Zero headroom means the top was touched; an overflow that wraps the stack pointer cannot be measured reliably. Results are a sanity check, not proof of a worst-case bound.
