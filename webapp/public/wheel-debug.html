A 3–16 click action should produce exactly that many complete press/release cycles with Left only. Press duration is at least 8 ms; the next press follows release by at least 200 ms (roughly 208 ms or longer from press to press). The log also includes browser click, auxclick and dblclick notifications; these do not increase the cycle count. Manual mouse clicks in the area also count, so clear the log before testing.

Expected for a single held key at step 1: approximately 100–108 ms between events on either axis (about 10 per second), with possible extra USB or browser delay. Larger configured steps emit several individual wheel reports in a batch, so use step 1 for this check.

This records browser wheel and mouse events, not raw USB reports. Event gaps compare events of the same type and mouse button within a burst, so an immediate press/release or a double-click notification does not skew the scroll interval. The OS/browser can change the wheel delta or combine events. “Event gap” uses event.timeStamp; “Receipt gap” uses performance.now() when the handler runs. If receipt times bunch up but event times do not, browser delivery is delayed. Pixel deltas are host-scaled movement, not firmware wheel counts. Other mice and trackpads can also generate events; their device identity is unavailable here. Buttons are Left, Middle and Right; the buttons mask is 1=Left, 2=Right, 4=Middle. CSV includes both clocks, all deltas, button masks, press duration, browser click detail, and modifier keys.
